Cookies and Tracking

Last updated: September 1, 2023

Overview

You can review and adjust your personal data processing settings and/or withdraw consent that you have granted in connection with cookies and other tracking technologies at any time.

We use the CMP provided by OneTrust B.V., of 82 St. John Street, Farringdon, London, EC1M 4JN, United Kingdom ("OneTrust"), to manage user consent for both our website and our app.

What categories of cookies does BLINDLEE use?

  • Strictly necessary cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to work and cannot be disabled in your systems. They are used for login sessions during watch parties.
  • Functional cookies: These cookies enable the website to provide advanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your subtitle preferences or volume levels.
  • Performance cookies: These cookies enable us to count visits and traffic sources so that we can measure and improve the performance of our watch party synchronization.
  • Marketing cookies: These cookies may be set through our website by our advertising partners.

Managing your cookies

With the exception of strictly necessary cookies, we only use cookies with your consent. You can manage cookie settings on our website using our CMP or by changing your browser settings.
